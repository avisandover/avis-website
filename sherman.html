Sherman Reservation

History

Acquired in 2004, the property consists primarily of a 3.7-acre, open meadow bordered by trees—an uncommon landscape in Andover. The former owners, wishing to ensure its permanent preservation, partnered with AVIS to protect the land. To provide access from Haggetts Pond Road, AVIS also purchased a 50-foot-wide strip through the adjoining house lot, along with a portion of the wooded land at its rear. In total, approximately 5.5 acres were acquired.

Natural features

The property includes a scenic meadow and an adjoining wooded area enriched by mature plantings established by the previous owners, who were dedicated gardeners. Along the approach to the meadow, a variety of trees and ornamental species reflect their care and long-term stewardship.
