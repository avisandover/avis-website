Andover Trails Guide, 7th Edition (2026)

The updated Andover Trails Guide is now available from local merchants and at town events. It is a collaboration of the various conservation groups throughout Andover.

This freshly updated 2026 edition of the Andover, Massachusetts trails guide provides maps, guides, descriptions, and histories of more than 62 unique and interesting conservation properties. These span thousands of acres of forests, meadows, streams, and ponds. It also covers the many volunteer groups and official organizations that are dedicated to establishing and promoting Andover’s trails and conservation land. Whether you are an experienced Andover hiker or new to the area, this guide is your starting point for easily accessing and enjoying the beauty of Andover’s open spaces.

The Andover Trails Guide, 7th edition (2026) is available for $20 at the following locations:

  • Andover Bookstore, 74 Main Street, Andover
  • Butcher Boy Market, 1077 Osgood Street, North Andover
  • Rocky's Ace Hardware, 50 Peters Street, North Andover
  • Barron's Country Store, 429 Lowell Street, Andover
  • Oak and Iron Brewing Co, 18 Red Spring Road, Andover
